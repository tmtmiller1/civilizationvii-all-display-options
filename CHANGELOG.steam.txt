==== 1.0.0 | 2026-06-25 | src 9e46166c47dc | gen 520a2686b6a2 ====
[b]Version 1.0.0 · 25 June 2026[/b]

[b]Added[/b]
• [b]Resolution (all modes).[/b] Every standard 16:9 and 16:10 resolution up to your display's native size, including modes the base game's dropdown leaves out.
• [b]Global UI scale slider (50–200%).[/b] The engine's full UIGlobalScale range, versus the built-in slider's 50–125% clamp. Lower = smaller HUD, more visible map.
• [b]UI auto-scale toggle.[/b] Turn off the post-patch auto-sizing that can look “zoomed in” and control the size yourself.
• [b]One-click, device-aware presets.[/b] Detects your panel and offers Current / Recommended / Maximum zoom-out / Game default.

[b]Notes[/b]
• Options live under the native Options screen's “Mods” category (and render in Mod Settings Manager when present). Good-citizen by construction: no shared localStorage writes, and all UIScripts are uniquely named so they can never shadow another mod's option modules.
